Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information Collection

PGlove collects minimal information necessary for its operation. As an experimental browser, we are committed to protecting your privacy. PGlove may collect:

  • Browser usage data (page visits, session data)
  • Technical information (IP address, device type, OS)
  • Crash reports and error logs

2. Data Usage

Collected data is used solely for improving PGlove, debugging, and providing the best user experience. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

PGlove may integrate with third-party services (GitHub, Codeberg, NPM, NuGet). These services have their own privacy policies. Please review them separately.

4. Cookies

PGlove may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device unless you explicitly opt into cloud features. PGlove does not store your personal data on remote servers.

6. Security

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or delete any personal data collected by PGlove. Contact us at pglove@atomicmail.io.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
