WHAT'S COMING

v1.0.4 Features

Four brand-new additions to make PGlove feel like your childhood Game Boy.

🔴

Pokéball UI

Every icon reimagined. Tabs become Pokéballs, buttons snap like the original red-and-white design, and menus have that classic catch animation.

🎨

Custom Icons

A complete icon set inspired by the original 151 Pokémon. Each one hand-drawn in the 8-bit pixel style, ready for every action in the browser.

🎵

Nostalgic Sounds

Level-up chimes on new tabs. A catch jingle on successful searches. The heal sound when a page loads. Optional, but unforgettable.

⚡

Type-Based Themes

Switch between Fire, Water, Grass, Electric, and Psychic color schemes. Your browser, your typing, your style.
